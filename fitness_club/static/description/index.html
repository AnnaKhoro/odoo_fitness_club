Fitness Club Management

Members, trainers, schedule and attendance in one Odoo app

Manage your fitness club end-to-end: contacts and personal data of members, subscription plans, group classes, trainers and visit tracking.

Key features

  • Members with profile, contact data and full subscription history.
  • Tariff plans with duration, price and visit limit (3 demo plans included).
  • Subscriptions with automatic state transitions (Active / Expired) based on dates.
  • Group classes in calendar / kanban with capacity and trainer assignment.
  • Attendance tracking with anti-duplicate constraint and active-subscription validation.
  • Wizards: mass-renew subscriptions; attendance report by period.
  • PDF Member card with colored statuses for the visit history.
  • Security: two cascading groups (User → Admin) inside a single privilege.
  • Ukrainian translation out of the box.
